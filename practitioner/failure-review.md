## Failure chain

1. **Trigger:** What condition initiated the problem?
2. **Detection:** Who or what first detected it?
3. **Interpretation:** Was the state understood correctly?
4. **Response:** Who acted and what did they do?
5. **Recovery:** How was operation restored?
6. **Residual risk:** What remains unresolved?

## Reliability-first questions

- Did the system fail safely?
- Was the failure visible to the correct role?
- Did the user have sufficient authority to respond?
- Was the recovery procedure known?
- Did recovery require an unintended specialist?
- Would the same failure likely recur on another shift?
