## Citation discipline

RAISE-HRI follows three simple rules:

1. **Do not cite a paper only because it is relevant to the topic.** Cite it for a claim it actually supports.
2. **Separate conceptual and experimental evidence.** A framework paper should not be used to support a specific physiological result unless it reports that result.
3. **Do not force every `.bib` entry into the manuscript.** An uncited but tangential reference is better removed than inserted in the wrong context.
